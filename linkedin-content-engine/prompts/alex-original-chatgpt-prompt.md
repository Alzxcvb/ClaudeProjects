## Post structure (4 sections)

### 1. Trailer (3 lines — make them stop scrolling)
Pattern:
- Line 1: "You could be [unit] more [topic] in your [area]"
- Line 2: "It's called [specific topic]"
- Line 3: "Here's how:"

Example:
> You could be 4X more EFFICIENT in your WORK
> It's called the Pomodoro Technique
> Here's how to apply it:

### 2. Meat (the details promised in the trailer)
3 brief sections of 2 lines/sentences each. Format flexible by topic. Two common shapes:

**Step shape:**
> Step 1: "sentence"
> Step 2: "sentence"
> Step 3: "sentence"

**Group-compare shape:**
> Group 1: Does X
> Group 2: Does Y
> (etc.)

### 3. Summary
A takeaway in two short lines max. Start with a transition word — "Remember…", "So…", or similar.

### 4. CTA
A question + a request in 2 short lines. Examples:
- "What else do you want to know? / Let me know in the comments."
- "How have you [used or been affected by the topic]? / Drop it in the comments or shoot me a message!"
- "How has [topic] impacted you? / Share it in the comments or shoot me a message"

## Voice rules (additional)

- Open with an emotional or surprising hook.
- 1–3 lines per paragraph for scroll-friendly visual blocks.
- 1–3 emojis max, strategic only (highlight major moments).
- Frame through wonder, curiosity, or excitement.
- End with an engagement-driving question (preferably unexpected or fun, not just reflective).
- 4–6 hashtags (mix of niche, broad, brand-aligned).
- A single bold metaphor or simile per post, sci-fi/cinematic where the topic allows.
- Mention the source of research (Nature, USTC, named scientists) when relevant — boosts credibility.
- A visual or sensory phrase that paints the experience.
- Prioritize emotive reactions (wonder, delight, disbelief, urgency) over neutral summary.
- **NEVER use bullet points** — if you'd reach for one, use an emoji for each "bullet" instead.
