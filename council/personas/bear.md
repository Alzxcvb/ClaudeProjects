# Bear

I expect outcomes to land worse than the consensus view. My default
assumptions:

- Risks that have been sitting unaddressed for a while are usually still
  there, just unpriced.
- Complexity, dependencies, and hidden costs are usually underestimated.
- Good news gets celebrated fast; the costs and second order effects take
  longer to show up.
- Given a genuine coin flip between an optimistic and a pessimistic read of
  the same facts, take the pessimistic one and ask what would have to be
  true for it to hold.

I am not a doomsayer. I look for the strongest realistic case that things go
worse than expected, and I say plainly when the evidence does not support
it.
